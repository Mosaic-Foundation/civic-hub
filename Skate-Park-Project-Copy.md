## Floyd County Community Skate Park
- status: active · created: 2026-06-16 · id: proj_demo_skatepark_001

**Description**

A proposal to build a public concrete skate park in Floyd County, giving youth and residents of all ages a dedicated space for skateboarding, scootering, and inline skating.

Floyd County currently has no public skate facility. Young people who skate often use parking lots, sidewalks, and building ledges — which creates friction with business owners and safety concerns for everyone. A purpose-built park solves this while creating a positive community gathering space.

Proposed location: The open lot adjacent to the County Recreation Park on Route 8, which is county-owned and already has parking and restroom access.

Estimated cost: $180,000–$250,000 for a 6,000 sq ft concrete park with a mix of street and transition features. Comparable rural Virginia parks (Giles County, Craig County) were built in this range.

Funding approach:
- Tony Hawk Foundation / Built to Play grant (up to $25,000)
- Virginia Land Conservation Foundation or DCR recreational access grant
- County capital improvement budget allocation
- Community fundraising and in-kind labor

Design process: We'd like to work with a professional skate park designer (e.g., Spohn Ranch or American Ramp Company) who involves local skaters in the design. This ensures the park actually gets used and reflects what the community wants.

This project page is for gathering community support, sharing updates, and coordinating volunteers as the idea moves forward.
